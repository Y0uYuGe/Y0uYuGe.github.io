# When a Vehicle Blocks the Way
First step on the brake or let the speed drop naturally; when you estimate the car is about to stop, press the clutch and shift to 1st gear, then press the brake, then start off with half-clutch; after starting, press the accelerator and shift to 2nd gear, then maintain the speed. If you estimate the car will not need to stop, wait until the distance is appropriate and then press the accelerator to set off. (Generally, stopping is better.)
# Preparation
Get in the car, fasten the seat belt, adjust the seat and mirrors, check the lights, the handbrake, and the gear position, flash the high/low beam alternately twice, then press the clutch and brake, turn the key all the way, and then wait for the light test.

# Lights
Overtaking, curves, arch bridges, crosswalks, ramps: alternate high and low beams.
Poor lighting: high beam.
Parking by the roadside: position lights (small lights).
Following another vehicle, meeting an oncoming vehicle, signal present, good lighting: low beam.

# Starting Off
Turn on the left turn signal, press the brake and clutch, shift to 1st gear, release the handbrake, release the brake, slowly release the clutch completely, then move your left foot aside, press the accelerator; when the speed reaches 15, press the clutch, shift to 2nd gear, release half the clutch, then release the other half, press the accelerator, and stabilize in 2nd gear.

# Gear Up and Down
Press the accelerator to 25, press the clutch fully, shift to 3rd gear, press the accelerator to 35, press the clutch, shift to 4th gear, press the accelerator and maintain 40-45 for about 5 seconds, then press the brake, slowly decelerate to 25, release the brake, then press the clutch, shift to 2nd gear, release half, then release the other half, and then continue pressing the accelerator.

# U-turn
Turn on the left turn signal, release the accelerator, let the car travel a distance and slowly decelerate; when the speed reaches 10, press the clutch and shift to 1st gear, while pressing the brake with your foot to lower the speed a bit more; when the speed is slow enough, release the brake, slowly release the clutch, while also pressing the brake with your foot when the opportunity arises to reduce speed; after the car passes the zebra crossing, turn the steering wheel fully to the left; after the car straightens out, directly release the clutch and press the accelerator; when the speed reaches 15, release the accelerator, press the clutch, shift to 2nd gear, release the clutch halfway, then halfway again, press the accelerator, and stabilize the speed in 2nd gear.

# Pulling Over to the Side
Turn on the right turn signal, press the brake, decelerate to 10, release the brake, press the clutch fully, shift to 1st gear, do not release the clutch, press the brake again to control the speed, and at the same time watch until the right side of the windshield wiper just touches the right edge line; after it has touched for a while, press the brake to stop the car; keep the right turn signal on the whole time — turn it on again if it goes off; straighten the wheel first, then pull in to the curb.

# Getting Out of the Car
Keep the right turn signal on, shift to neutral, pull the handbrake, turn off the engine, release the brake, release the clutch, observe the rearview mirror, open the door slightly to check behind, then close the door.
# Overtaking
Turn on the left turn signal, count three seconds, observe the rearview mirror, press the accelerator to speed up and turn left; after straightening out, turn on the right turn signal; after three seconds, press the accelerator to speed up and turn right; after straightening out, turn off the right turn signal and continue driving in 2nd gear.
# Driving Straight
Keep the right side of the windshield wiper right next to the leftmost edge of the right line; while the instruction is still being announced, adjust the position of the wiper relative to the right line until the steering wheel is straight and this distance no longer changes; after the instruction finishes, fine-tune the steering wheel by less than 5 degrees to keep the position of the wiper relative to the right line unchanged; at the same time press the accelerator to 35, then release the accelerator, let the speed drop to 25, and then keep pressing the accelerator to maintain it.
# Turning Left
First turn on the left turn signal, change lanes, release the accelerator, let the car decelerate naturally; when the speed reaches 10, press the clutch and shift to 1st gear; if you still need to stop at this point, press the brake; after the light turns green, use half-clutch; after starting off, fully release the clutch and then start pressing the accelerator, keeping the speed still below 20; after the car leaves the zebra crossing, turn the steering wheel half a turn to the left; after the car enters the road, straighten the steering wheel, accelerate to 20, press the clutch, shift to 2nd gear, and continue driving.
# Turning Right
Similar to turning left, except the turning intersection is different.
# Bus Stop
After the body of the car enters the bus stop area, press the brake.
# Crosswalk
When the front of the car is still some distance from the crosswalk, press the brake once.
