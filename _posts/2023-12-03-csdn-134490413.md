# Traversals of Trees and Forests
## Pre-Root Traversal of Trees
Similar to the preorder traversal of binary trees: root, left, right
The corresponding binary tree is the tree represented by the child-sibling method, and the preorder traversal of this binary tree is the same as the pre-root traversal of the original tree
![Image](/images/csdn/2023/csdn-134490413/image-051-f2c7a466e6.png)
## Post-Root Traversal of Trees
Similar to the postorder traversal of binary trees: left, right, root
The corresponding binary tree is the tree represented by the child-sibling method, and the inorder traversal of this binary tree is the same as the post-root traversal of the original tree
![Image](/images/csdn/2023/csdn-134490413/image-052-27201e8627.png)
## Level-Order Traversal of Trees
Post-root and pre-root traversals are depth-first traversals
Level-order traversal is a breadth-first traversal
![Image](/images/csdn/2023/csdn-134490413/image-053-e4d91f9a02.png)
