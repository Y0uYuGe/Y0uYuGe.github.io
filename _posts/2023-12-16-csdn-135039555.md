# CISC and RISC

 A stored-program computer consists of a circuit plus storage components
![Image](/images/csdn/2023/csdn-135039555/image-002-3e9733ff16.png)

The memory-accessing instructions differ
RISC is more flexible because many functions are not fixed and are written by yourself
Because CISC instructions take different amounts of time to execute, implementing an instruction pipeline is more difficult
Because CISC places no restrictions on memory-accessing instructions, the process is less cumbersome and fewer auxiliary registers are needed
