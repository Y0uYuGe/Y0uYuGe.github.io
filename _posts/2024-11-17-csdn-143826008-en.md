# Attacks on RSA
In all cases, e and n (the public key) are known

During enumeration, after assuming x, possible values of q are chosen based on (p+q)/2 > sqrt(a), such that when q equals y, n=(x+y)*(x-y) holds

If the absolute value of p-q is large, after assuming a value p you need to enumerate many possible q values before finding the correct p and q
![在这里插入图片描述](/images/csdn/2024/csdn-143826008/image-017-8f69b5286d.png)
Keep encrypting until the result matches c (mod b)
![在这里插入图片描述](/images/csdn/2024/csdn-143826008/image-018-8192bc7034.png)

A common-modulus attack can ultimately leak the plaintext

The low-exponent attack uses the Chinese Remainder Theorem to recover m^e
![在这里插入图片描述](/images/csdn/2024/csdn-143826008/image-019-8e922c0f35.png)
