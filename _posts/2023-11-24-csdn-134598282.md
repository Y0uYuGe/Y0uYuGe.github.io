# External Storage
Mechanical hard drives, i.e., disks
## Disk Storage
  Writing uses electric current to generate a magnetic field to write to the disk
  Reading uses the electric field generated when the magnetic carrier moves to obtain data
  Magnetic materials are susceptible to interference from external magnetic fields
  In the figure below, the front-to-back order of N and S on the magnetic carrier represents the corresponding stored binary bits
  ![Image](/images/csdn/2023/csdn-134598282/image-002-342e01ca6d.png)

 Magnetic material is coated circle by circle onto the drive shaft platters
 ![Image](/images/csdn/2023/csdn-134598282/image-003-0dcc478543.png)
 A cylinder is the cylindrical surface formed by the tracks of each recording surface
![Image](/images/csdn/2023/csdn-134598282/image-004-5fb0d4329d.png)

![Image](/images/csdn/2023/csdn-134598282/image-005-545fbe2180.png)
## Disk Performance Metrics

Formatted capacity is generally smaller than unformatted capacity, because in formatted capacity some sectors need to serve as backups for other sectors 
![Image](/images/csdn/2023/csdn-134598282/image-006-4aa81ef79a.png)
Each track records the same amount of information, but since the innermost track has the highest bit density, its manufacturing technology is the most advanced
![Image](/images/csdn/2023/csdn-134598282/image-007-27ba090e7d.png)
The disk controller time is the time from when the signal is sent until the disk controller begins to respond
Transfer means rotating the recording surface to read out the data of the corresponding sectors

## Disk Array
A disk array can be understood as storing different parts of a whole dataset onto different disks
![Image](/images/csdn/2023/csdn-134598282/image-013-2903d3ca15.png)
 
Redundancy means whether there are backups; checking means verifying whether the data has changed
![Image](/images/csdn/2023/csdn-134598282/image-014-c053619443.png)![Image](/images/csdn/2023/csdn-134598282/image-015-3f1c13d4a8.png)
RAID2 uses the Hamming code checking method, so compared to RAID1, fewer disks are needed for checking and redundancy
![Image](/images/csdn/2023/csdn-134598282/image-016-9d45fd91f4.png)
The higher the RAID level number, the higher the security and reliability
