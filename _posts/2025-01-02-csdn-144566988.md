Imitating physical handwritten signatures is insecure in the digital world, because a physical handwritten signature can be understood as inserting a digital signature after an electronic document; since copying is possible in the digital world, one could copy the signature to forge it. Therefore, every electronic document must be tightly bound to its signature — the plaintext and the private key together serve as the input to the signature function, which creates the tight binding
