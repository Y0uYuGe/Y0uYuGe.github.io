## Overview

AI_Study_Note is my working collection of AI learning notes, reference links, and code experiments. The repository includes machine-learning fundamentals, convolutional networks, reinforcement learning, language-model notes, and ROS workspace material. Examples include LeNet, VGG, CNN image classification, and transfer learning; the embodied-AI section also collects reading links for CLIP, DINO, and SAM.

## Explore the material

Start from the [repository](https://github.com/Y0uYuGe/AI_Study_Note), choose a topic, and read its Markdown notes beside the associated Python files. The material is organized by subject, with setup depending on each example's datasets, libraries, or ROS environment.

The collection combines learning records, reference material, and individual experiments, with depth varying by topic.
