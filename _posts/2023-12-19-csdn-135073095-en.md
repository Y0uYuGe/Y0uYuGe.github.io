# Microprogrammed Controller
## Design Idea of the Microprogrammed Controller
  Here one micro-instruction corresponds to a series of micro-operations, whereas the earlier hardwired controller has one beat corresponding to a series of micro-operations
  ![在这里插入图片描述](/images/csdn/2023/csdn-135073095/image-019-e7fe184cb8.png)
## Basic Structure of the Microprogrammed Controller
After the CPU fetches an instruction, the opcode of the instruction is sent to the micro-address formation unit. The micro-address formation unit generates the initial micro-address, then reads the micro-instruction corresponding to that initial micro-address into the CMDR, and the operation-control part of the micro-instruction issues multiple control signals (one micro-instruction corresponds to multiple micro-commands, and one micro-command corresponds to one output line). The next-address field is then passed to the sequencing logic, which decides the address of the next micro-instruction based on the flag bits
![在这里插入图片描述](/images/csdn/2023/csdn-135073095/image-020-9da3c34d45.png)
## Working Principle of the Microprogrammed Controller

First execute the microprogram of the fetch cycle to bring the instruction into the IR (the next instruction is obtained from the next-address field of the last instruction of the previous execution-cycle microprogram)
The sequencing logic determines whether to skip the indirect addressing cycle based on the addressing-mode bits of the address code in the IR
Then the opcode in the IR passes through the micro-address formation unit to obtain the initial micro-address
