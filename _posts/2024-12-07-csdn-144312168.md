# Elliptic Curve Cryptosystem and Its Operations
## Principle
[Public-Key Encryption Technology: Principles of the ECC Elliptic Curve Encryption Algorithm](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1BY411M74G/?spm_id_from=333.337.search-card.all.click&vd_source=a1be939c65919194c77b8a6a36c14a6e)
 Elliptic curve = ellipse + curve 

- Elliptic curves are symmetric about the x-axis
- The transformation is performed through point G a number of times equal to the value of the private key
- Transformation method: first time: take the tangent at point G, reflect the intersection point, connect the reflected point with G, and reflect the new intersection point; remaining N times: connect the current reflected point with G again, take the resulting intersection point, reflect it, connect it with G, and reflect the new intersection point again
