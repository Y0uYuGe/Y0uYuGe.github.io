## Dedicated Data Path Approach
Interfaces like C1 are connected to the CU, which issues control signals to make them active or inactive (whether they can pass through)
![在这里插入图片描述](/images/csdn/2023/csdn-135069833/image-009-02b0ac79ea.png)
