Similar to a counter, it increments at a fixed frequency corresponding to the hardware

After power-on it is automatically reset to 0, done automatically by the hardware

# mtimecmp
 Needs to be set manually; it is also 64-bit
The initialization we implement sets the corresponding mtimecmp to an initial value based on the hartid, then sets some interrupt enable bits
![在这里插入图片描述](/images/csdn/2024/csdn-137860421/image-004-cf0e44121a.png)
# Mechanism
mtime increments at a certain frequency; when it is greater than or equal to mtimecmp, a timer interrupt is generated

MTIE is the M-mode timer interrupt enable bit in mie
MTIP is the bit in mip for M-mode that indicates whether a timer interrupt is currently being sent
