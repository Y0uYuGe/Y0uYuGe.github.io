![Image](/images/csdn/2024/csdn-143672960/image-033-9c549750a1.png)


Proof of the Euclidean algorithm
- d1 is the greatest common divisor of a and b, so it is also a factor of r, and therefore d1 is also a common factor of b and r
- d2 is the greatest common divisor of b and r, so it is also a factor of a, and therefore d2 is also a common factor of a and b
- d1 is less than or equal to d2 (d1 is a common factor of b and r)
- d2 is less than or equal to d1 (d2 is a common factor of a and b)
- Therefore the two are equal
