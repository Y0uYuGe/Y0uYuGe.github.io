## Overview

A collaborative knowledge base documenting security incidents involving AI coding tools and their software supply chains. Cases connect the affected tools, attack paths, impact, and supporting sources.

## My contributions

- Researched and documented cases involving LiteLLM, Nx, Gemini CLI, Cursor, and Cline.
- Added structured YAML metadata, attack-chain diagrams, and links to advisories and incident reports.
- Archived reference pages to preserve the evidence behind case analyses.
- Submitted additional case studies concerning Claude Code and GitHub Copilot in Visual Studio.

## Scope

My work focuses on case research and documentation grounded in cited advisories and published incident reports. The broader report is maintained collaboratively by Narwhal Lab and its contributors.
