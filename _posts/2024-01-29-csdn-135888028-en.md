## Process Switching and Its Procedure
Process scheduling in the narrow sense is selecting a process to run from the ready queue; the selected process may be the one just suspended and put back on the ready queue, in which case no process switch is needed because it is still the same process. If it is another process, a process switch is required (because it is a different process)

"Save" means saving into the Process Control Block (PCB)
"Restore" means reading the corresponding information out of the PCB and restoring it

The higher the switching frequency, the higher the degree of concurrency
