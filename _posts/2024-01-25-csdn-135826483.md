# Distortion
Simply means the result differs from the original
Two cases

The first three are positively correlated; the last one is negatively correlated
![Image](/images/csdn/2024/csdn-135826483/image-001-f072a79873.png)

The higher the symbol transmission rate, the more severe the distortion. Possible reasons include:

1. Increased signal frequency components: as the symbol transmission rate rises, the signal's frequency components increase accordingly. High-frequency signals are more susceptible to circuit characteristics, such as the response of resistors, capacitors, and inductors, which may change the signal waveform and produce distortion.
2. Channel bandwidth limitation: according to the Nyquist criterion, to avoid intersymbol interference the symbol transmission rate has an upper bound, namely the channel's maximum bandwidth. If the transmission rate exceeds this limit, signals interfere with each other and distortion results.
3. Signal attenuation and noise interference: as the transmission rate rises, signal attenuation in the transmission medium also intensifies, and noise has an even more significant impact on the signal. All of these factors can degrade the signal quality at the receiver and increase the risk of distortion.
# A Phenomenon of Distortion - Intersymbol Interference
Analog signals are continuous; the telephone channel is an analog channel
The signal bandwidth of an analog signal is a frequency
Signal bandwidth is not equal to channel bandwidth
200 Hz cannot pass because it is easily affected by other high-frequency signals, causing such heavy attenuation or interference that it may even disappear, so it cannot pass
4000 Hz cannot pass because its oscillation frequency is too high: the time interval becomes so short that the boundaries between symbols can no longer be distinguished — in other words the symbol transmission rate is too fast to tell symbols apart, or equivalently the symbol length is so short that the recognition time while passing is too brief to be processed in time
It's like a crowd of people walking past continuously — can you distinguish each individual? — versus people walking past one at a time, where you can recognize each person clearly
Slow transmission rate

Both the high and low levels are symbol signals

# Nyquist and Shannon
Nyquist: reduces the signal interference between symbols internally
Shannon: a limit imposed under the influence of noise — it is external

When both formulas apply, compute each and take the smaller value; the smaller one guarantees both conditions are satisfied
