# Double-flag Check-after Method
When process P0's while loop has executed halfway and switches to process P1, P1 will also enter its while loop; when process P1's while loop has executed halfway and switches back to process P0, P0's while loop will still continue to execute — switching back and forth like this, but the clock is stuck in each process's while loop.
