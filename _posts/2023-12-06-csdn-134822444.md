## Indirect Addressing
(A): the content stored at address A
Two-level indirect addressing determines whether further indirect addressing is needed by checking whether the highest bit of the fetched address content is 1 or 0

The address range of indirect addressing is the range representable by the content at the address corresponding to the operand (i.e., the range representable by one memory word)
