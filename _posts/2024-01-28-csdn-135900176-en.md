# Notes
Fasten the seat belt
The only situation requiring one and a half turns is when exiting after reversing into the garage.
Two places require the turn signal

# Preparation
First fasten the seat belt, then turn the car key 90 degrees, and adjust your seat position until, leaning back with your head looking straight ahead, you just barely cannot see the car's hood. Adjust the mirrors. Finally, press the clutch and brake all the way down, turn the key another 90 degrees, and the car starts. Release the handbrake and shift into first gear. Release the clutch.

# Reversing into the Garage
Drive with the midpoint of the lower edge of the left rearview mirror aligned with the left line.
When your seat is roughly over the boundary line ahead, press the clutch all the way down and brake
Then, while reversing, when the tire seen in the left rearview mirror presses on the line, turn the wheel fully to the right. When the white line disappears, turn back half a turn. Keep reversing; once the car body is straight, check whether your seat is exactly over the mark. When exiting, turn one and a half turns to the left; once the mirror is roughly out of the garage, shift into reverse, keep the wheel still — on the way back you may hold it fully turned for a moment — then continue reversing with one and a half turns. Once the car body is straight, check whether your seat is exactly over the line (the line in the middle of the seat)

# Parallel Parking
Drive with the lower-left corner of the front mirror aligned with the left line; when you can no longer see the front line, look at the right rearview mirror. When the diagonal corner is centered, shift into reverse; when you see the third black line (counting the leftmost black as one), straighten the wheel, then back into the space. Once the wheel presses on the line, start turning the wheel — one and a half turns to the left. After the car body is straight, turn on the signal, shift gears, keep the wheel still, and once the left rear wheel crosses the line, straighten the wheel and turn one turn to the right

# Curve Driving
Enter with the lower-left corner of the front mirror aligned with the left line; when you notice the left side of the left rearview mirror leaving the left curve, turn one turn to the left and keep it until the curve changes direction, then hold the original turn until you can no longer see the line through the door, then drive straight; when the bottom of the right rearview mirror shows the right curve, turn right one turn plus 45 degrees. Drive on, and once the wheel has returned to center, go straight

# Straight Driving
Enter with the lower-left corner of the front mirror aligned with the left line. You may turn on the left signal early; wait until the corner line is roughly at the middle of your seat (or the point between your two parted thighs aligned with that line), then turn fully to the right

# Hill Start and Stopping
On the way up, the right side of the wiper's highest point should roughly hug the right line; once on the slope, switch to the left side of the wiper's highest point roughly hugging the right line. When the line disappears and the third line appears slightly below the left rearview mirror, stop. Pull the handbrake, release the handbrake, slowly release the clutch until the car trembles, then release the brake while loosening the clutch a bit more so the car moves forward and up
