I'm **Liu Like**, known online as **Y0uYuGe**. I am an undergraduate student. My interests include **endpoint security**, **AI security**, **embodied AI**, and **AI infrastructure**.

My learning and projects cover vulnerability analysis, reverse engineering, binary exploitation, intrusion detection, operating-system internals, AI systems security, robotics, and infrastructure for AI applications.

## Focus Areas

- **Endpoint Security**: vulnerability analysis, reverse engineering, binary exploitation, and systems security practice.
- **AI Security**: security of AI systems and applications.
- **Embodied AI**: robotics, perception, control, and the connection between AI and physical systems.
- **AI Infrastructure (AI Infra)**: model deployment, inference systems, containers, and infrastructure for AI applications.

## Selected Projects

- [MagicSkills](https://github.com/Narwhal-Lab/MagicSkills) (Narwhal-Lab): reusable skill infrastructure for AI agents, with shared skill collections, AGENTS.md synchronization, and CLI/Python integration.
